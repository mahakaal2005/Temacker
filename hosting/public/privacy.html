Temacker

Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Temacker ("the app") helps small teams pass tasks between people. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: your name, email address, profile photo and a Firebase user ID, when you sign in with Google. They identify you and show teammates who holds a task.
  • What you create: projects, roles, tasks, hand-offs, requests and activity entries you add or act on. This is the app's core function, and it is shared with the members of the project it belongs to.
  • A notification token for your device, so the app can tell you when a task is handed to you.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): stack traces, device model, Android version, app version and your Firebase user ID. They are used only to find and fix bugs.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): screens viewed, app version, approximate region and a pseudonymous app instance ID. They are used only to understand how the app is used and improve it.

We do not collect your contacts, location, photos or files, and the app shows no ads.

How it is stored and shared

  • Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, Analytics). It is encrypted in transit.
  • Project content is visible to the members of that project, according to their roles.
  • We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Export: in the app, go to You → Your data → Export to download your project data as JSON or CSV.
  • Delete: see Delete your account.

Children

Temacker is not directed at children under 13.
