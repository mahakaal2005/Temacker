Temacker

Delete your Temacker account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Temacker and go to the You tab.
  2. Tap Your data.
  3. Tap Delete my account and data, then confirm.

If you lead a project, the app first asks you to hand leadership to someone else, so the team isn't left without a Leader.

Without the app

Email atul.k.singh5002@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, asking for your account to be deleted. We will do it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your Temacker sign-in (your Firebase account) and your membership in every project.
  • Your device's notification token.

What is kept

  • Tasks, hand-offs and activity entries you created inside a team's project stay with that project, because they are part of the team's shared history. They keep the display name they were created with.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics are kept by Google Firebase for its standard retention period (up to 90 days for Crashlytics, up to 14 months for Analytics) and are not linked to an active account.
