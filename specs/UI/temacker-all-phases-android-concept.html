What this concept commits to

Nothing in the token layer changed from Phase 1 — same navy, paper, amber, teal and coral, same radii, same two typefaces, same warm hairlines. Eleven new components were added and the icon set grew by sixteen glyphs on the same 24dp grid at 1.9 stroke. Where a Phase 1 rule and a nice idea disagreed, the rule won.

Colour rules, unbroken

AmberInteraction and state. In-flight batons, overdue, stale offers, active tab, FAB, meters.
TealIdentity and classification only. Granted roles, accepted trail nodes, the member count meter.
CoralDestruction only — three appearances in 30 screens: the delete-task menu item, the delete dialog, the delete-my-account button. Sign out is not one of them.
Not coralOverdue tasks, stale offers, declines, and the record of a deletion. A late task has destroyed nothing.

Craft floor

TypeNothing below 10.5px anywhere, including mono micro-labels. Roboto 400/500/700, Roboto Mono 400/500.
TargetsIcon buttons 44dp, buttons 56dp, list rows 80dp, notification actions 38dp inside a 13px-padded card.
ContrastEvery text pair clears 4.5:1 on its own surface — amber-ink, teal-ink and coral-ink exist so an accent can be spoken as well as shown. Amber and teal are light by design and cannot reach 3:1 on paper, so every accent fill carries a 1px ink boundary (5.9:1 and 5.7:1) and no accent is ever the only thing telling you what happened: the in-flight ring has a chip, the load bar has its number, the selected row has a check.
LayoutStatus 30 + app bar 60 + tabs 50 + nav 66 + gesture 22 inside 380×824dp. FAB clears the nav bar by 16.

Three places a Phase 1 promise overruled a better-looking idea. Delete stays permanent because the roles screen already printed "permanent — cannot be undone", so accountability moved to an append-only event log instead of a soft archive. Overdue chips are amber, not coral, because coral was reserved for destruction and a late task destroys nothing. And the non-transferable Leader rule was not bent for the annual club handover — a cycle ends and a new project inherits the roster, so the rule and the history both survive. One open question is still yours to answer: whether succession is the flow you want, or whether a club should be able to hand the Leader role over directly. The screens above assume succession.
